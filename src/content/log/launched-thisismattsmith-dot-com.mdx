The site is built with [Astro](https://astro.build/) and builds off an open source [template](https://github.com/Spikeysanju/sanju.sh) by [Sanju](https://sanju.sh/). I'd already started working on this site and their were two main things I wanted to do with it: 

1. have a place for [writing](/writing/), i.e., blog posts I've spent a bit of time on, and 
2. a feed of unedited, more "raw" [thoughts](/thoughts/) (like my own little social feed)

I came across Sanju's site at the perfect time because it had both of these already created and done really well. I also thought the *ship* section of his site was a clever idea so I adapted it into my [log](/log/).

I'll continue to adapt the site for my own needs and update the design to personalise it to my own style. However, I love the clean, minimalistic design of the original project.
