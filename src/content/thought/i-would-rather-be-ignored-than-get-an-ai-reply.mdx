I'm getting AI replies to my posts. I'm getting AI replies to my questions on other people's posts.

The responses are boring and superficial and sometimes just plain wrong. It doesn't do anything for my perception of that person and it doesn't help me at all.

I know some people treat it as a numbers game but they're losing more than they're getting. 

They lose the opportunity to sit with their thoughts and come up with their own ideas. 

They lose the potential for deeperunderstanding and a chance to further their relationship with the topic. 

They let their intellectual curiosity atrophy. 

They lose respect of people like me.
